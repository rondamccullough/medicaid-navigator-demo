To: Radiant Motive LLC

From (organization):

Subject: Exploratory interest in reviewing Radiant Motive Navigator

acknowledges receipt of information about Radiant Motive Navigator, a non-clinical, browser-based guide being developed by Radiant Motive LLC. The Navigator is designed to organize changing healthcare and public-benefit program information into clear, repeatable steps and direct individuals to official resources and enrollment assistance.

The current Maryland preview illustrates guided information pathways related to Medicaid notices, work/activity information, possible exception pathways, renewals, proof instructions, appeals and local assistance referrals. It is an evaluation preview, not a production eligibility system. It does not determine eligibility, diagnose medical frailty, certify exemptions, submit applications or documents, access patient accounts, or replace Maryland Health Connection or clinic staff.

Non-binding expression of interest

is open to considering further exploratory discussion with Radiant Motive LLC about whether the concept may be relevant to its work involving Medicaid member education, patient navigation, care coordination, referral handoffs or related services. This statement records interest in discussion only; it does not confirm that the organization has evaluated, approved, endorsed, selected or agreed to use the technology.

No obligation or transfer of responsibility

This letter is non-binding and does not:

  • require a purchase, license, payment, funding, procurement, pilot, implementation or deployment;
  • require staff time, patient or member participation, data access, data sharing, integration or operational support;
  • create a partnership, joint venture, agency, vendor relationship or contractual commitment;
  • constitute an endorsement, certification, compliance determination or representation that the preview is production-ready; or
  • transfer any clinical, eligibility, regulatory, legal, privacy, security or Medicaid administrative responsibility to the organization.

Any future design evaluation, pilot, data exchange, procurement or deployment would require separate written agreements and the organization's normal legal, privacy, security, clinical, compliance and procurement reviews, as applicable. A proposed no-charge, 30-day design evaluation at up to three mutually agreed sites may be discussed separately; signing this letter does not enroll the organization in that evaluation. Any paid license would also require a separate written agreement.

Either party may end exploratory discussions at any time. Neither party may use the other's name, logo or this letter publicly without separate written permission.
