# South Carolina module — official source register

Content review date: 2026-11-22. This is a source register for the South Carolina demonstration module, not a legal opinion or a promise that future policy dates will not change.

## Retroactive Medicaid eligibility

- SCDHHS Medicaid Advisory Council packet, Nov. 10, 2026, pages 8–11: current policy described as up to three months before the application month; Section 71112 change described as up to two months; effective date Jan. 1, 2027.  
  https://www.scdhhs.gov/sites/dhhs/files/11.2026%20MAC%20Packet.pdf
- CMS CMCS Informational Bulletin, Nov. 18, 2026: applications made on or after Jan. 1, 2027; one-month limit for the Medicaid adult group and two months for most other eligibility groups; eligibility must have existed when services were furnished.  
  https://www.medicaid.gov/sites/default/files/2026-11/CIB111826-Summary-medicaid-CHIP-enrollment-related-CIB-slides.pdf
- The application experience therefore does not promise a universal two-month look-back. It instructs users to confirm their eligibility category and case with SCDHHS.

## Managed care Phase II and long-term care waiver

- SCDHHS Phase II page: Phase II will transition HCBS waiver, nursing-facility and hospice services into managed care; consolidation of Community Choices, HIV/AIDS and Mechanical Ventilator Dependent waivers into Healthy Connections–Long Term Care.  
  https://www.scdhhs.gov/partners/managed-care/managed-care-initiatives/phase-ii-progress
- SCDHHS public notice, Nov. 2, 2026: proposed HC-LTC waiver effective date July 1, 2027; the three waiver programs are proposed to be consolidated; current participants are not expected to be adversely impacted.  
  https://www.scdhhs.gov/communications/waiver-application-healthy-connections-long-term-care-waiver
- SCDHHS Nov. 2026 Medicaid Advisory Council packet: July 2027 is a preliminary target contingent on CMS approvals, overall MCO and system readiness, and coordinated outreach.
  https://www.scdhhs.gov/sites/dhhs/files/11.2026%20MAC%20Packet.pdf
- The module describes July 2027 as a target, not a guaranteed go-live date.

## Phase I and dual-eligible coordination

- SCDHHS states Healthy Connections Prime ended Jan. 1, 2026; members were encouraged to transition to a D-SNP, subject to individual rules and exceptions.
  https://scdhhs.gov/members/managed-care-plan-information/healthy-connections-prime-mmp
- SCDHHS D-SNP overview:
  https://www.scdhhs.gov/partners/managed-care/dual-special-needs-plans-d-snp
- SCDHHS final action notice explains Phase I enrollment of dual-eligible members, certain waiver members and nursing-facility residents in MCOs for Medicaid State Plan services; waiver and nursing-facility services remained fee-for-service at that stage.
  https://www.scdhhs.gov/communications/public-notice-final-action-managed-care-enrollment-carve
- The module does not state that every member must select the same carrier for D-SNP and Medicaid MCO. It directs users to verify coordination, networks and authorizations with both plans.

## Use limitations

- The guide does not determine eligibility, submit applications, review patient accounts, diagnose medical frailty, certify an exception or store patient documents.
- Do not enter PHI or identifying information. The website host may process ordinary technical logs.
- The demo analytics panel is simulated in-browser only. Public reporting remains disabled.
- Review official sources before each production content release.
