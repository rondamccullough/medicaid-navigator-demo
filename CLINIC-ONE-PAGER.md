# Radiant Motive Navigator
## A consistent, link-based Medicaid navigation path for clinic teams

**Access exists. Navigating access is the challenge.**

Radiant Motive Navigator is a non-clinical, browser-based guide that organizes changing Medicaid information into clear, repeatable steps. It helps patients understand what a notice may be asking, review possible state-listed exception pathways, prepare to follow official instructions, and find the right enrollment assistance.

### How a clinic can use it

1. **Share one link** through an existing patient text, portal message, email or QR code.
2. **Patients self-navigate** through a guided path using the available language controls; some detailed content remains in English and is flagged in the interface.
3. **The guide explains next steps** and directs patients to Maryland Health Connection, their official notice or local enrollment assistance.
4. **Staff have a common reference** for routine questions and referral handoffs.

### What the current preview includes

- Guided pathways for notices, work/activity information, possible exceptions, renewals, proof instructions and appeals.
- A Maryland county directory for local enrollment assistance.
- Links to official Maryland Medicaid and Maryland Health Connection resources.
- Clinic-branded preview routes for configured demonstration organizations.
- A browser-based experience that does not require an EHR integration to evaluate.

### What it does not do

- Determine Medicaid eligibility or guarantee coverage.
- Diagnose medical frailty, certify an exception or make clinical decisions.
- Ask for or submit patient names, Medicaid IDs, diagnoses, medical records or documents through the guide.
- Submit forms or documents to Maryland.
- Replace Maryland Health Connection, a provider, a caseworker or a legal adviser.
- Provide automated SMS delivery, patient-level tracking, analytics dashboards or EHR integration in the current preview.

### Proposed 30-day design pilot

Up to three clinic sites can evaluate patient comprehension, staff usability, referral handoffs and operational fit. The pilot uses aggregate staff tallies and feedback; no patient-level data is needed. There is no automatic conversion to a paid license.

### A responsible policy message

Maryland has delayed enforcement of the new federal Medicaid work requirements through June 30, 2027. Other eligibility and enrollment changes scheduled for January 1, 2027 are not delayed. The delay is a preparation period—not a guarantee that coverage will continue after that date. Members should read official notices and complete renewals by their stated deadlines.
