# Radiant Motive Navigator — Maryland Clinic Go-to-Market Plan

## 1. Positioning

**A reusable navigation layer for complex public-benefit access pathways.**

For Maryland clinics, Radiant Motive Navigator is a link-based, non-clinical guide that helps patients understand Medicaid changes, review possible state-listed exception pathways, prepare to follow official instructions and connect with enrollment assistance. It complements Maryland Health Connection; it does not replace it.

**Core message:** Access exists. Navigating access is the challenge.

Avoid selling the product as a medical-frailty decision tool, eligibility engine, document repository, EHR integration or coverage-loss prevention guarantee. Those are not current capabilities.

## 2. Why now — without a false July 1 cliff

Maryland announced on November 20, 2026 that it will delay enforcement of the new federal Medicaid work requirements through June 30, 2027. The state says other federal eligibility and enrollment changes scheduled for January 1, 2027 will still be implemented. Members may still be asked to verify whether they meet the work rule or qualify for an exception.

The correct commercial frame is **a preparation and navigation window through June 30, 2027**. Do not promise that all work-requirement disenrollments will begin on July 1, 2027, or that Navigator use prevents disenrollment. The timing and implementation details remain subject to official state and federal guidance.

Some adults may face more frequent renewals. Maryland's consumer Check-In materials describe six-month renewals for some adults; direct each member to their own official account and notice rather than predicting an individual deadline.

## 3. Initial ideal customer profile

Prioritize organizations where Medicaid questions, renewal navigation and referrals are a recurring operational burden:
- Federally Qualified Health Center (FQHC) organizations.
- Community health centers and multi-site primary-care groups.
- Behavioral-health and substance-use treatment organizations.
- Health-care-for-the-homeless and other safety-net providers.
- Clinic networks with patient-access, enrollment-assistance or community-health-worker teams.

**Buyer roles:** COO, executive director, VP/Director of Operations, patient access leader, community health worker manager, Medicaid enrollment lead. Compliance/privacy/security reviewers are essential approvers, not optional late-stage contacts.

## 4. Maryland market sizing

The National Association of Community Health Centers' January 2026 Maryland fact sheet lists 16 health-center organizations and 161 service-delivery sites. This is a useful starting point for account mapping, not a guarantee that every site is a qualified buyer or that all sites can be sold independently.

A 100-site target would represent about 62% of those 161 reported service-delivery sites. That is an ambitious scale target and should be treated as a multi-stage objective, supplemented by other qualified clinics—not as a near-term forecast.

## 5. Offer and pricing hypothesis

**Proposed introductory site license:** $200 per clinic site per month, or $2,400 per site per year.

| 25 | $5,000 | $60,000 |
| 50 | $10,000 | $120,000 |
| 100 | $20,000 | $240,000 |

The $200 price is a hypothesis to validate through pilot conversion and buyer interviews. Do not state that it fits within a clinic director's discretionary budget or bypasses procurement; purchasing authority varies by organization.

A proposed 10-site bundle at $1,750 per month would produce $17,500 MRR / $210,000 ARR at 100 sites. It is a 12.5% discount from list price. Do not offer this discount until implementation and support costs are understood. At that bundle rate, 120 sites would be needed to exceed $20,000 MRR.

Online subscription checkout is not enabled in the current preview. Initial pilot and license agreements should be handled through a written scope and invoice until billing operations are implemented.

## 6. Pilot-led sales motion

Start with three anchor clinic sites—not a mass rollout.

**30-day design pilot:** up to three sites, no charge, no automatic conversion. Test:
- Patient comprehension of the guidance.
- Staff ability to use the same pathway consistently.
- Quality of referrals to Maryland Health Connection and local assistance.
- Content gaps, accessibility and language limitations.
- Operational fit and willingness to pay.

Use aggregate staff tallies and structured feedback. Do not invent completion rates or coverage outcomes. The current preview has no patient-level analytics or reporting dashboard.

**Conversion gate:** a paid deployment requires a written scope, clinic privacy/security review, agreed support expectations, and confirmation of the production data flow.

## 7. Sales sequence

1. Build an account list of Maryland FQHCs and other safety-net clinic networks.
2. Prioritize three organizations with an identifiable patient-access or enrollment-assistance owner.
3. Request a 20-minute workflow discovery meeting; do not lead with a broad partnership request.
4. Show the clinic-branded preview and the member pathway.
5. Provide the clinic one-pager, security/privacy fact sheet and pilot plan before asking for a pilot decision.
6. Secure written approval for a 30-day design pilot.
7. Review findings with the executive sponsor and compliance lead.
8. Convert only when the clinic confirms operational value and approves the production scope.
9. Expand site-by-site within the organization after a successful first-site evaluation.

## 8. Differentiation

Position Navigator as a **complement** to:
- Maryland Health Connection and state resources, which remain authoritative for eligibility, notices and submissions.
- EHR and revenue-cycle systems, which serve clinical documentation, billing and operational functions.
- Human enrollment assisters and community navigators, who handle individual case details and complex situations.

The Navigator's current distinction is a lightweight, reusable guided workflow and local referral layer. Do not claim that competitors lack telemetry, that enterprise platforms always cost thousands per month, or that deployment is guaranteed in 24 hours unless those statements are independently substantiated.

## 9. Claims sales staff may use

- "A link-based, non-clinical Medicaid navigation guide."
- "Helps patients understand common next steps and find official assistance."
- "Designed to complement Maryland Health Connection, not replace it."
- "The current preview does not ask for patient names, Medicaid IDs, diagnoses or document uploads."
- "No EHR integration is required to evaluate the current link-based preview."
- "Clinic-branded preview routes are available for configured demonstration organizations."

## 10. Claims sales staff must not use

- "Zero-PHI," "HIPAA exempt," "no BAA required" or "no security review required."
- "Prevents disenrollment," "guarantees coverage" or "stops July 1 coverage losses."
- "Automatically identifies medical frailty" or "certifies exemptions."
- "Collects or submits provider documentation."
- "24-hour production deployment" or "no procurement approval required."
- "Real-time clinic analytics," "completion tracking" or "monthly reporting dashboard" in the current preview.
- "Fully translated in five languages." Detailed content remains incomplete in some languages.

## 11. Go-to-market readiness gates

Before a paid production deployment, complete:
- Full browser and mobile QA across every pathway and language.
- Independent policy review and a dated update process.
- Human translation and review of all member-facing content in every advertised language.
- Accessibility review, including keyboard navigation, screen-reader labels, contrast and mobile layouts.
- Privacy and security review of Netlify hosting, access logs, retention, network requests and any future analytics.
- Written clinic data-flow diagram, service terms, support boundaries and incident-response process.
- Legal review of HIPAA role and BAA requirements for each proposed deployment.
- Authenticated tenant administration before enabling clinic reporting or private dashboards.
- Billing, invoicing, cancellation, support and renewal procedures.

**Current status:** public evaluation preview suitable for guided demonstrations and a carefully scoped design pilot after clinic approval. It is not yet an enterprise-ready, analytics-enabled, HIPAA-certified or self-service subscription product.
