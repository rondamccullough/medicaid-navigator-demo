# Radiant Motive Navigator — 30-Day Clinic Design Pilot

## Purpose

Validate whether a guided, clinic-branded Medicaid navigation link improves patient understanding, staff consistency and referral handoffs. The pilot is a workflow evaluation—not a clinical study, eligibility assessment or promise of coverage retention.

## Proposed scope

- Duration: 30 days.
- Sites: up to three agreed clinic sites.
- Cost: no-charge design pilot, subject to a signed pilot scope.
- Participants: designated clinic operations lead, front-desk or patient-access staff, and volunteer reviewers.
- Data rule: no patient-level data, names, Medicaid IDs, diagnoses, medical records or document copies.

## Before launch

1. Identify the clinic executive sponsor and day-to-day site lead.
2. Obtain clinic approval from privacy, security, compliance and communications as required.
3. Agree on the clinic display name, approved distribution channels and exact pilot URLs.
4. Confirm that staff will not enter patient information into the preview.
5. Provide a short staff orientation and escalation instructions.
6. Agree on aggregate measures and who will maintain the tally.

## Pilot workflow

**Week 1 — Set up and baseline**
- Confirm the current state-policy content and review date.
- Share the clinic preview link with designated staff.
- Record baseline staff confidence in answering common Medicaid navigation questions.
- Review the official-state handoff and local-assistance directory.

**Weeks 2–3 — Use and observe**
- Staff may share the link through existing clinic-approved channels.
- Collect staff feedback on confusing language, missed pathways and referral usefulness.
- Use a simple aggregate tally sheet: number of links shared, number of staff-assisted referrals, and number of times a user asked for additional help.
- Do not record patient names, member IDs, diagnoses, exact notice details or other identifying information.

**Week 4 — Evaluate**
- Repeat the staff confidence check.
- Review aggregate counts and workflow observations.
- Identify content changes, accessibility issues and missing referral resources.
- Decide whether to stop, revise or negotiate a paid deployment.

## Measures

Use only aggregate, non-identifying measures agreed in advance:
- Number of times staff shared the link.
- Number of staff-reported referrals to Maryland Health Connection or local assistance.
- Staff-reported time or effort needed to answer routine navigation questions.
- Staff confidence before and after the pilot.
- User-reported clarity gathered without names or case details.
- Number and type of content corrections requested.

Do not publish a percentage completion rate, coverage-retention result, reduced denial rate or time-savings claim unless the pilot actually measures it with an approved method and the result is documented.

## Deliverables

- Configured clinic preview label and link.
- Staff quick-start instructions.
- Agreed aggregate tally template.
- End-of-pilot findings summary with limitations.
- Written recommendation: stop, revise, extend or convert to a paid scope.

## Boundaries

The Navigator does not determine eligibility, diagnose medical frailty, certify an exception, submit documents, access patient accounts or guarantee coverage. Maryland remains the authority for eligibility and deadlines.

The pilot does not include automated SMS, EHR integration, patient-level analytics, a reporting dashboard, formal security certification, a BAA or a production service-level agreement unless separately scoped and approved in writing.

## Conversion discussion

The proposed introductory price is $200 per site per month ($2,400 per site annually). Conversion requires a separate written agreement, clinic privacy/security approval, agreed support scope and confirmation of the production data flow. There is no automatic conversion or automatic billing.
