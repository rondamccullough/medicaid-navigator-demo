For context only: 80 hours per month averages about 18.5 hours per week over a 52-week year.

The calculation uses annualized averages: weekly × 52 ÷ 12; every two weeks × 26 ÷ 12; twice monthly × 2; monthly × 1. Actual state reporting periods and rules may differ. Do not count the same time twice.

How to use the Medicaid hours calculator

  1. Enter the number of hours in a typical period.
  2. Select whether those hours occur weekly, every two weeks, twice per month, or monthly.
  3. Review the estimated monthly total and keep your own supporting records.
  4. Ask your state which activities and reporting periods it recognizes before relying on the estimate.
