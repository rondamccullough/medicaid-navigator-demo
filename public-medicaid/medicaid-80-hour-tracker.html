Free Medicaid planning tool

Medicaid 80-hour monthly activity planner

Use this free tool to organize activity hours that may count under the federal Medicaid community-engagement framework. It adds your entries in your browser and compares them with the 80-hour monthly planning reference.

Important: The requirement does not apply to every Medicaid member. CMS describes multiple pathways, including qualifying activities, monthly earnings of at least $580, and half-time education. Your state must confirm whether the requirement applies to you, which activities count, and what evidence it accepts.

This tool runs in your browser. It does not send or store your entries, determine eligibility, verify qualifying hours, or establish compliance. Confirm all rules with your state Medicaid agency.

How to use this planner

  1. Enter the hours you completed or expect to complete in each activity category.
  2. Use separate categories only when the hours do not overlap.
  3. Enter monthly earnings if you want to compare them with the current CMS public-guide reference.
  4. If you are enrolled at least half-time, check the education box; your state determines the applicable definition and documentation.
  5. Print the summary for your own records, then ask your state what it accepts.
