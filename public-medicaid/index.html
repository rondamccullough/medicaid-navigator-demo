Frequently asked questions

Important things to know.

Does the 2027 Medicaid community-engagement requirement apply to everyone?

No. It applies to defined groups of adults under the federal law, with exclusions and exceptions. Other Medicaid coverage groups are not automatically subject to it. Check the official notice and your state Medicaid agency.

Does 80 hours mean I must work 80 paid hours?

No. The federal framework describes multiple qualifying activity pathways, including certain work, community service, work programs, and education. The CMS public guide also describes a monthly earnings pathway. Which activities count and how they must be documented should be confirmed with your state.

Does a six-month hold-harmless period mean I can ignore Medicaid notices?

No. The optional period relates to certain denials or disenrollments based solely on community-engagement noncompliance during an approved period. Other eligibility rules, renewals, and notice deadlines still matter.

Can Radiant Motive tell me whether I qualify or whether I am exempt?

No. This website provides general education and navigation tools. It does not make eligibility determinations, assess personal exemptions, submit forms, or replace a state agency or qualified assister.

Will the website include SNAP and Section 8?

Yes. The platform is being built around reusable program modules. Medicaid is the first interactive module. SNAP, Housing Choice Vouchers (Section 8), and veterans' benefits are included in the expansion roadmap, with official resources available in the program directory.
