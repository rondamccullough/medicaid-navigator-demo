Medicaid agency call preparation sheet

Before you call your state Medicaid agency or a qualified enrollment assister, use this checklist to organize what you want to ask. A prepared call can help you understand the request, identify the right next step, and keep a record of the response.

Privacy reminder: This worksheet is for your own use. Do not enter your Medicaid ID, Social Security number, date of birth, password, or other sensitive information on this website. If the agency needs to verify your identity, follow its official secure process.

Selections and notes stay in this browser and are not sent to Radiant Motive. The agency's official instructions control. Ask for the representative's name or ID, the date of the call, and any reference number the agency provides.

During the call

  • Ask the representative to explain any unfamiliar term or request.
  • Write down the date, the instructions, and any deadline.
  • Ask how to submit information and how to confirm receipt.
  • Ask where to find the instructions in writing.
  • Repeat the next steps back to confirm you understood.
