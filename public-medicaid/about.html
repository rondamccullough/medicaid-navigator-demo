About this resource

Clearer navigation for complex public programs.

Radiant Motive LLC develops reusable navigation technology intended to help people understand complex public-benefit and healthcare access pathways. This public Medicaid Access Guide is an independent educational resource designed to help readers understand general changes, find official state information, and organize practical next steps.

What this guide does

  • Summarizes publicly available federal and state information in plain language.
  • Shows state status records with source links and review dates.
  • Provides general navigation checklists and links to official agencies.
  • Separates public education from Radiant Motive's paid institutional FQHC Clinic Track.

What this guide does not do

  • It is not a government website and is not affiliated with CMS or a state Medicaid agency.
  • It does not determine Medicaid eligibility or whether an exemption or exclusion applies to an individual.
  • It does not submit applications, renewals, documents, or reports to a state agency.
  • It does not replace official notices, legal advice, or help from a qualified assister.
