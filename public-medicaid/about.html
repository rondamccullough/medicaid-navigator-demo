About this resource

Clearer navigation for complex public programs.

Radiant Motive LLC develops reusable navigation technology intended to make complex healthcare and public-benefit access pathways easier to understand and complete. The Public Benefits Navigator is an independent educational resource that organizes public information into practical next steps and directs visitors to the government agencies responsible for official decisions.

What is available now

  • Medicaid 2027 state-information tracker with review dates and source links.
  • Interactive next-step plans for common Medicaid situations, including notices, renewals, applications, and questions about upcoming changes.
  • Browser-only activity-hour and pay-frequency calculators, a renewal checklist, a notice deadline calculator, a notice response planner, and an agency call preparation sheet.
  • Links to CMS and official state Medicaid resources.

Expansion roadmap

The platform is designed around reusable program modules. Medicaid is the first interactive module. SNAP, Housing Choice Vouchers (Section 8), veterans' benefits, and other public programs are planned for future releases. Program rules and official resources will be maintained separately so that updates can be made without rebuilding the entire navigation experience.

What this guide does not do

  • It is not a government website and is not affiliated with CMS or a state Medicaid agency.
  • It does not determine eligibility, exemptions, exclusions, or compliance.
  • It does not submit applications, renewals, documents, or reports to an agency.
  • It does not replace official notices, legal advice, or help from a qualified assister.
  • It does not request patient records, Medicaid IDs, or uploaded benefit documents.

Separate institutional product

This free public resource is separate from Radiant Motive's paid FQHC Clinic Track, which is designed for healthcare organizations and participating clinic locations.
