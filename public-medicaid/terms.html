Official instructions control

Public-benefit programs are administered by federal, state, tribal, territorial, and local agencies. Rules, forms, deadlines, notices, and implementation plans may change. Always follow the latest official notice and instructions from the agency responsible for your case. Contact that agency or a qualified assister for help with your circumstances.
