Free notice organizer

Medicaid notice response planner

Receiving a Medicaid notice can be confusing. Use this organizer to identify the general type of notice, record the date printed on it, and create a list of practical next steps and questions for your state agency.

Important: Do not ignore a notice or rely on this tool to calculate a legal deadline, determine appeal rights, or decide whether you remain eligible. Follow the instructions and response date in the official notice. If the notice describes an appeal or fair hearing, use the instructions printed on it.

Before you respond

  1. Read every page of the notice, including any attachments.
  2. Identify the action requested and the response method.
  3. Record the response date exactly as printed.
  4. Contact the agency promptly if you do not understand the request.
  5. Keep a copy of anything you submit and the confirmation.
