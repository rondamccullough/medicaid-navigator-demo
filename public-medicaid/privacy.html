Privacy

Privacy notice

Effective date: November 27, 2026

The Public Benefits Navigator is designed to provide general information without requiring an account or collecting patient-level information. Do not enter sensitive personal information into this website.

Information you enter into the tools

The state selector, activity-hour planner, and deadline calculator run in your browser. The values you enter are used only to calculate and display results in the current page session. This version does not send those entries to Radiant Motive, save them to a server, create a profile, or upload documents. Refreshing or closing the page clears the tool entries.

Do not enter your name, Medicaid ID, Social Security number, date of birth, diagnosis, detailed income records, work history, passwords, or copies of notices or benefit documents.

Technical information

The website host may process standard technical information needed to deliver, maintain, and protect the website, such as IP address, browser and device details, request timestamps, and security logs. The exact information retained depends on the hosting provider and configuration. No analytics service is enabled in this build.

Advertising and cookies

Advertising is not enabled in this build. If services such as Google AdSense or analytics are added, those providers may use cookies or similar technologies to serve, measure, or personalize ads and analyze traffic. Before activation, this notice, the consent experience, and any required disclosures must be updated to match the actual services and applicable requirements.

External links

This guide links to CMS, Medicaid.gov, USDA, HUD, VA, and state or local government websites. Those sites have their own privacy policies and practices. When you leave this site, the external agency's terms and privacy practices apply.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact contact@radiantmotivenavigator.com.
