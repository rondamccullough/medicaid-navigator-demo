Medicaid renewal checklist

Use this checklist to organize the steps that commonly matter before and during a Medicaid renewal. Check off each item as you complete it. Your selections stay in this browser and can be printed.

Important: Your state Medicaid agency's renewal notice controls. A federal community-engagement hold-harmless period does not pause all renewal requirements or deadlines.

Need help with a notice?

Read the entire notice, note the response date, and use the phone number or website printed on it. If you disagree with a decision, follow the appeal or fair-hearing instructions in the notice.
