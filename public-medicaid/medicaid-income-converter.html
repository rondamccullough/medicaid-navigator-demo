Why pay frequency matters

People may be paid weekly, every other week, twice per month, or once per month. Those schedules produce different paycheck counts over a calendar year. Converting to a monthly average can help organize records, but it does not replace the method a state uses to calculate countable income.

Questions to ask an enrollment assister

  • Which Medicaid coverage category am I applying for or enrolled in?
  • Which household members and income sources are included for that category?
  • Should I report gross pay, net pay, or another amount?
  • How should overtime, variable hours, self-employment, or irregular income be reported?
  • What documents or pay periods does the agency need?
